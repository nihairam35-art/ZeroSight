C. Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to key technical questions about zero-day detection mechanics.

Traditional cybersecurity tools rely on static signatures and CVE databases. Because zero-day attacks have never been documented before, signature scanners miss them entirely. ZeroSight uses an unsupervised deep autoencoder neural network trained exclusively on baseline legitimate network behavior.

When an attacker executes an unseen vector, the packet reconstruction error spikes far above normal thresholds. The detector flags this mathematical divergence in under 5 milliseconds—detecting the attack by its behavioral novelty rather than an existing signature.

Risk scores (scaled from 0 to 100) are generated through an ensemble scoring function that combines:

  • Autoencoder Reconstruction Loss (40% weight): Magnitude of statistical departure from normal traffic patterns.
  • Packet Rate Anomaly (25% weight): Flow acceleration compared against rolling host baselines.
  • Target Port & Protocol Entropy (20% weight): Rarity of the destination service and socket transition sequences.
  • Payload Structural Divergence (15% weight): High-entropy bytes indicating shellcode, obfuscated SQLi, or encrypted payloads.

Navigate to the Zero-Day Simulator via the sidebar. Select an attack category (such as DDoS, WebAttack, or PortScan) from the dropdown, then click Launch unseen attack.

ZeroSight streams simulated attack traffic directly through the detector. You will observe the real-time catch rate meter update, the interactive SVG donut chart animate, and the live detection feed record each caught packet stream with millisecond timestamps.

In AI-driven threat detection, precision and recall represent the core balance of SOC efficacy:

  • Recall (91.5% in ZeroSight): Out of all actual attacks occurring on the network, what fraction did the system catch? High recall ensures that zero-day intrusions do not slip past unnoticed.
  • Precision (94.2% in ZeroSight): Out of all alerts raised by the system, what fraction were genuinely malicious attacks rather than benign noise? High precision prevents analyst alert fatigue.

D. Troubleshooting & Diagnostics

Self-diagnostic verification suite and solutions for common operational issues.

Detection Engine Issues

If the engine reports Degraded, verify that your network tap or mirror port is streaming valid Ethernet frames. Ensure DPDK kernel drivers are loaded.

Model Configuration Issues

If false alarms spike after network topology changes, recalibrate the baseline threshold in Settings from 85 up to 90 to accommodate elevated background traffic.

Missing Alerts in Dashboard

Verify your active filter criteria on the Dashboard and Alerts views. Low-severity background anomalies (scores < 40) are logged directly to the Incident Audit.

Simulator Injection Errors

If attack launch stalls, check that another simulation is not currently running. The generator limits concurrent injections to preserve telemetry integrity.
